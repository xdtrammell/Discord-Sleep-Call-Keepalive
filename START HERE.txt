SLEEP CALL KEEPALIVE v1.1.0
============================

A small Windows utility for Discord sleep calls that disconnect after long
periods of computer inactivity. It sends F15 only after at least 165 minutes
of Windows input inactivity and only while a supported Discord app is running.
It does not change your mic, mute state, or Discord installation.

QUICK START

1. Extract this ZIP to a normal folder.
2. Double-click Install.cmd and wait for "Installation complete".
3. Run Check Status.cmd. Look for 40 bytes (expected 40).
4. Run Test F15 Input.cmd once, while you are not gaming.
5. Leave the computer awake during overnight calls. The display may turn off.
6. Next morning, Open Log.cmd and look for a SENT entry.

The ZIP includes the C# source, editable settings, full README, and uninstaller.
No admin rights, AutoHotkey, game list, or always-running process is required.

IMPORTANT: The executable is unsigned. Windows Smart App Control can block it
and has no individual-app exception. Do not turn off your PC's protection just
for this program unless you independently choose to accept that tradeoff.

This is not an official Discord product or a guarantee against every call drop.
